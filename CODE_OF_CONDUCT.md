# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers, assume good intent, give specific and kind feedback, and accept it the same way. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, or open a private security advisory on this repository for anything sensitive. Reports are handled in confidence.
